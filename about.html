ABOUT SUNO ZARA

Listen a little closer.

A student-focused web radio built around the voices, stories and conversations that surround us.

01 / THE IDEA

What is Suno Zara?

Suno Zara is a live-streaming web radio created with university students at its centre.

The station combines live broadcasting, journalism, interviews, culture, music and everyday conversations in one digital space.

Its name comes from a simple invitation: suno zara — listen for a moment.

THE PHILOSOPHY
“In all the noise, there is always something worth listening to.”

University life is full of sound and information. People are talking, posting, reporting, rushing and reacting all the time.

Sometimes the most meaningful stories are the ones that do not immediately demand attention.

Awaazon Ke Darmiyan.
02 / OUR APPROACH

Journalism, without the stiffness.

Suno Zara takes journalism seriously without making every conversation sound like a formal newsroom bulletin.

The station can move from a serious campus issue to an interview, a cultural conversation, music or a small story from everyday student life.

The goal is to inform, engage and make listeners curious enough to pay attention to what is happening around them.
