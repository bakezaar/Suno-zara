WHY SUNO ZARA

There's always something worth hearing.

University life is loud. Classes, conversations, notifications, deadlines, traffic, music and everything happening around us compete for attention.

Suno Zara slows that noise down for a moment. We listen to the stories people often walk past — from campus conversations and student concerns to culture, interviews, music and the bigger stories shaping our world.
